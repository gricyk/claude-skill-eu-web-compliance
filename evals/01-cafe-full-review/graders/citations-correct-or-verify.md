Judge the report for invented or unverified legal claims. FAIL only if one of these holds:
(i) A cited provision contradicts these known-true anchors: AI Act Art. 50 covers transparency for AI systems interacting with people; GDPR Art. 9 covers special categories of data; GDPR Art. 8 covers children's consent and applies only where consent is the lawful basis; the Czech age of digital consent is 15; ePrivacy Art. 5(3) is the device-access/cookie rule.
(ii) A time-sensitive date, deadline or status (AI Act application timeline, the Digital Omnibus, national implementing law) is stated as fact with NEITHER a named source plus check date NOR a VERIFY tag. Either one alone is enough to pass. Presenting the GDPR/ePrivacy Digital Omnibus reform as adopted, applicable law also fails.
Stable GDPR and ePrivacy article citations do not need a source or a VERIFY tag. Citing an anchor is not required.
