# AI Transparency Notice template, AI Act Art. 50

Use whichever section applies to the actual AI feature on the site, delete the rest.

---

## AI system disclosure

### If the site has a chatbot or conversational AI

Short notice placed at the start of the interaction, or visibly next to it:

> You are chatting with an AI assistant, not a person. {{optional_scope_note, e.g. "It can answer
> questions about our services and events, for anything else contact us at {{contact_email}}."}}

### If the site publishes AI-generated or AI-manipulated images, audio, or video

Visible label near the content:

> This {{image/audio/video}} was created or modified using AI.

Exception: if the content went through genuine human editorial review and a named natural or legal
person takes editorial responsibility for it, and it is not being presented as authentic depiction of
real events, the disclosure duty may not apply in the same way, `VERIFY` the current text of Art. 50
and any Commission guidance before relying on this exception, and prefer disclosure over omission when
in doubt.

### If the site publishes AI-generated or AI-assisted text as informational/news content

> This article was drafted with AI assistance and reviewed and edited by {{editor_or_organization_name}},
> who takes responsibility for its content.

If there was no meaningful human editorial review, disclose it plainly as AI-generated instead of using
the "reviewed by" framing above.

### If the site uses emotion recognition or biometric categorization

> This {{feature}} uses automated {{emotion recognition/biometric categorization}} technology.
> {{purpose_and_scope}}

Do not deploy this kind of feature on a children's or vulnerable-audience site without dedicated legal
review, flag this explicitly rather than drafting a notice and treating it as sufficient.
