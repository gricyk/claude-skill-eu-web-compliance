# National law guide

GDPR, the AI Act, and the DSA are EU regulations, directly applicable in all member states, but each
leaves specific points to national law, and the ePrivacy Directive is implemented differently country
by country. Do not embed country-specific facts here as static text, they date quickly and the risk of
stating a wrong one confidently is high. Instead, for the site's country of establishment (or, if
targeting a specific member state's public, that state), search for and confirm the following, citing
the source and date checked:

## Always check per country

1. **Age of a child's consent under GDPR Art. 8.** The default is 16, member states may lower it to as
   low as 13. Search: "[country] GDPR article 8 age digital consent". Directly relevant to any site
   with youth or children's programs.
2. **National supervisory authority (DPA)**, its current name, website, and complaint process. Needed
   for the privacy policy's "right to complain to a supervisory authority" section.
3. **National GDPR implementing act**, most member states have one covering DPO appointment rules,
   penalties, and procedural detail beyond the Regulation itself. Search: "[country] data protection
   act implementing GDPR".
4. **National ePrivacy/cookie transposition and current regulator guidance**, cookie enforcement style
   and guidance detail vary significantly by country (some DPAs publish very specific banner-design
   requirements).
5. **Legal-entity/website disclosure requirements ("impressum"-style rules)**, many member states
   require a specific operator-identification notice on commercial or even non-commercial websites,
   under national e-commerce or trade law, separate from the GDPR privacy notice. Search: "[country]
   website legal notice requirement imprint law".
6. **National AI Act competent authority/authorities**, designated separately by each member state.

## Example: Czech Republic, as a working template for the pattern above

Do not treat the following as settled, current fact, it illustrates the kind of thing to search and
confirm, current as of this writing and marked accordingly.

- Data protection authority: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (ÚOOÚ). `VERIFY` current contact details
  and any recent procedural changes.
- National GDPR implementing act: zákon č. 110/2019 Sb., o zpracování osobních údajů. `VERIFY` current
  consolidated text and any amendments.
- Age of a child's consent: commonly cited as 15 in secondary sources, `VERIFY` against the current
  text of the implementing act and any updated guidance before stating it in a report.
- Website operator disclosure: general commercial-communication and trade-law disclosure duties exist
  under Czech law (e.g. živnostenský zákon, obchodní zákoník provisions on business identification),
  `VERIFY` exact current requirements for the specific type of site (non-profit/church vs. commercial).

## How to use this in the workflow

When Step 1 of `SKILL.md` establishes the site's country, come back to this file, run the searches
above for that country, and fold confirmed results into the findings report with sources. Anything not
confirmed stays `VERIFY` rather than being asserted.
